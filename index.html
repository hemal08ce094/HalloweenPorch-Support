Hosting

How do I host a porch?

Open the Host tab and follow the four steps. Plot your porch on the map and drag the pin onto your door. Scan your sign and tap flat surfaces to place up to four AR scares. Choose a scare level, a riddle or a bat hunt, and a secret treat word. Then print the sign. Hosting needs you to be signed in to iCloud.

How do I print the sign?

In the Host tab, open Sign, then tap print. It prints at a fixed size on A4 or Letter paper, so print at 100 percent and do not choose "fit to page". Stick it flat at the steps, at about chest height, where your porch light falls on it. You can also share it as a PDF.

Can I show the sign on a screen instead?

Yes, for a test. For the night, a printed sign works best because screens glare and dim.

How do I switch my porch light?

Open Tonight in the Host tab and choose On, Low on candy or Off. Visitors see lit and low porches on the map. You can also use Siri or Shortcuts: say "Turn my porch light on in Halloween Porch".

What are the scare levels?

Little pumpkins has no jump scare and no sudden loud sound. Spooky has a ghost and spatial sound, with nothing sudden. Full fright includes one jump scare. Visitors see your level on the map before they walk over.

How do I delete my porch?

Open Settings and tap "Delete my porch". Porches are also removed after November 2. If you cannot get into the app, email us and we will delete it.

Visiting

How do I find a community?

Ask the organiser for the 6-character invite code, QR code or link. On the first screen choose "Join with a code" or scan the QR. An organiser creates a community with "Create a community" and shares the code from there.

The sign will not scan.

Check that the room is lit, hold the phone about an arm's length from the sign, and keep the whole sign in view. If it is too dark, the app says so. After a few seconds you can start the haunt without the sign.

Why does the map show no porches?

Porches appear when their hosts switch the light on or to low on candy. Check that you joined the right community. Without a signal the app shows the last copy it saved on your phone.

Where are my stamps?

In your passport inside the app. They are stored on your phone only, so deleting the app removes them.

Safety and reporting

How do I report a porch?

Open Settings, tap "Report a porch", pick the porch and give a reason. Reports go to the developer. The community organiser can also hide a porch independently. We review reports and remove content that breaks the rules within 24 hours.

Is it safe to walk with the phone?

The app is built so you do not look at it while you walk. It uses a heartbeat vibration that quickens as you near the porch, and AR opens only when you are at the steps. Stay on the pavement and watch for cars.
